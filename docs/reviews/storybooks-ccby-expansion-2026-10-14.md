## Mapping and deployment evidence

- 126 pages total; 104 story pages have a paragraph tap target, the matching source page audio clip, and matching follow-read text. Cover and license pages remain readable without synthesized mappings.
- The full-book narration remains available as chapter playback. Page taps use the separately published page recordings; no timestamps were guessed inside the full-book narration. The source audio was not manually listened to in this workflow; page-to-track matching follows the publisher’s per-page numbering and story-page transcript, and sound quality still needs a human spot check.
- 367 private page-image, thumbnail, full-narration, and page-audio files were uploaded (33,850,481 bytes). Independent cloud download/hash comparison passed 367/367.
- In the existing free nonproduction CloudBase, teacher task-reading directories and student reading catalogs showed all 11 books in each of two active fictional organizations. Every student detail read returned the full ordered pages; point-read/audio and follow-read readiness were true, and each paragraph audio map was returned page by page.
- These are nonproduction records only. No production content channel or mini-program release was changed.

## Deliberate exclusions

The CC BY-NC titles were excluded because their noncommercial terms do not fit this app's distribution use. “Grandma's bananas” and other long-page candidates need smaller, properly timed sentence segments; page-level tracks alone cannot support accurate sentence follow-read. “Magozwe” includes child abuse and homelessness and remains outside this batch pending an age-advisory path.

The official downloads page describes a mixed CC BY/CC BY-NC collection; this batch therefore checked the license on each selected work, not just the collection-wide page. Official source: [Storybooks Canada English collection](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/stories/en/), [downloads and license notes](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/downloads/), and [reuse FAQ](https://www.storybookscanada.ca/about/faq/).
